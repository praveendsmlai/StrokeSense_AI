## Scope to Verify

1. Human review of AI-generated minutes, decisions, actions and insights, including edits, approvals, rejections, reviewer identity, timestamps and audit history.
2. Publication workflow: only validated/approved outputs can be published; rejected drafts cannot become active; prevent duplicate publication.
3. Action management: ownership, status changes, due dates, SLA calculations, aging, overdue identification, closure rules and action-status history.
4. Notifications: publication notifications, reminders, due-date alerts, escalations, correct recipients and idempotency.
5. Auditability: verify persisted records for generation, review, approval, rejection, publication and action updates.
6. Authorization: verify BHR, BHR_LEADER and PLATFORM_ADMIN permissions and IBU-scoped access, including negative tests.
7. PRD-specific business rules and failure scenarios, including missing evidence, incomplete capture, invalid owners and unauthorized operations.
